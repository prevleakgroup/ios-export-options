Use the existing source structure in this repository as the reference point for the new design work.

## Brand direction
- Keep the overall look premium, professional, and modern.
- Follow the blue-and-white direction already present in the PrevLeak-inspired visual system.
- Use the existing logo language as the base for new logo concepts for PrevLeak Group and Saferide.
- Preserve a trustworthy fintech / mobility / innovation feel. 

## Logo requirements
- Create polished logo suites for PrevLeak Group and Saferide.
- Use blue and white as the primary visual language.
- Keep the design close to the existing PrevLeak Group logo structure so it feels familiar and credible.
- Add trademark treatment for all web and app surfaces, including website headers, footers, app names, and marketing sections.
- Deliver dark and light variants where practical.

## Recommended palette
- Deep navy: #07111f
- Primary blue: #4f8cff
- White: #f8fafc
- Teal accent: #2dd4bf
- Purple accent: #8b5cf6
- Gold accent: #fbbf24

## Website and app scope
- Four websites:
  - Main corporate site
  - Palettemath
  - Rider portal
  - Driver portal
- Two app entry points:
  - Android app
  - iOS app

## Trademark guidance
- Apply trademark styling consistently to brand names on websites and apps.
- Use a premium treatment such as ™ where appropriate for marketing and product surfaces.
- Keep the presentation clean and professional, not overly decorative.

## Prompt for GenSpark
Create premium brand assets and UI for Qvedic, PrevLeak Group, and Saferide using the existing repository source and current logo direction. Design modern blue-and-white logo suites that follow the existing PrevLeak-inspired style, add trademark treatment to all website and app branding, and produce polished landing page designs for four websites and two mobile app entry points. Use the palette: deep navy #07111f, primary blue #4f8cff, white #f8fafc, teal #2dd4bf, purple #8b5cf6, and gold #fbbf24.
