PrevLeak
Water, Sanitation, and Public Infrastructure Platform

PrevLeak is the flagship infrastructure intelligence platform within Prevleakgroup™. It helps municipalities, utilities, and public works teams coordinate responses around water, sanitation, and infrastructure risk.

The platform is built around a shared intelligence framework that combines machine learning, incoming data streams, reporting logic, and decision support. These capabilities help teams move from reactive operations to informed, evidence-led actions.

Key value:
- infrastructure incident visibility
- faster field response and service coordination
- clear audit trails and accountability
- stronger planning and municipal performance management

Business case:
PrevLeak creates measurable value by reducing operational blind spots, improving service delivery quality, and increasing the consistency of public infrastructure responses.
