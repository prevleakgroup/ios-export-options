Saferide
Mobility and Service Support Platform

Saferide is focused on improving rider and driver support with safer, more dependable mobility experiences. The brand provides a clear interface for communication, safety support, and service coordination.

Its value sits in the same shared intelligence architecture used by the wider group. Operational signals and service data are translated into better support decisions, stronger visibility, and more reliable service delivery.

Petrol surge feature:
- adaptive petrol surge support for rider and driver pricing confidence
- route efficiency guidance during fuel volatility periods
- transparent communication to reduce confusion when fuel costs spike

Key value:
- better support and communication
- clearer service coordination
- safer and more dependable mobility experiences
- improved operational oversight
- petrol surge resilience for both riders and drivers

Marketing audience focus:
- rider and driver audiences in the ride-hailing segment
- comparative content style aligned to user expectations from leading platforms such as Uber and Bolt
- performance messaging centered on trust, safety, and fuel-cost transparency
