About PrevLeak

Water, sanitation, and public infrastructure intelligence for modern municipalities.

PrevLeak focuses on the operational realities of water, sanitation, and public works teams. Its mission is to turn field events, public reports, and infrastructure signals into better service decisions, faster response, and more accountable delivery.

Our operating focus

We help municipalities and utilities manage incidents, service requests, and asset risk through connected reporting and evidence-led operations.

Our unique value proposition

Behind each experience sits a shared intelligence layer that combines data streams, reporting logic, and decision-support workflows. This allows infrastructure events, field activity, and public reporting to be translated into smarter actions.
