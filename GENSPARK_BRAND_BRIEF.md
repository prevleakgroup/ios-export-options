## Required direction
- Use the logos from the Google Drive account of thulaniprevleak@gmail.com and this business account as the base visual reference.
- Treat those logos as the authoritative source for color, form, visual identity, and brand tone.
- Do not replace the brand with unrelated styles or generic templates.
- Keep the final result premium, professional, and polished.

## Logo and brand requirements
- Create refined logo suites for Qvedic, PrevLeak Group, Saferide, Rider, Driver, and Palettemath.
- Use the business account logo colors and visual language as the foundation for each logo treatment.
- Preserve a clean, trustworthy, modern look appropriate for technology, mobility, and enterprise brands.
- Apply trademark treatment where appropriate on website headers, footers, app names, and marketing surfaces.
- Deliver dark and light variants where practical.

## Website and app template requirements
- Create a professional website template for each of the following products:
  - Main corporate site
  - Palettemath
  - Rider portal
  - Driver portal
- Create a professional app template for each of the following app entry points:
  - Android app
  - iOS app
- Each website template must include:
  - polished hero section
  - product overview
  - trust and credibility section
  - documentation or resources section
  - clear call-to-action buttons
  - mobile-friendly layout
- Each app template must include:
  - app icon concept
  - app store-style presentation
  - matching brand colors and typography
  - clear Android and iOS download messaging

## Visual system
- Keep the palette anchored to the business account brand colors from the Drive logos.
- Use deep navy, blue, white, and accent tones that complement the existing premium visual language.
- Maintain clear hierarchy, strong readability, and a modern SaaS / mobility / fintech feel.
