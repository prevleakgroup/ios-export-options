Create a complete social media registration and content rollout brief for the PrevLeak Group portfolio.

Brands to include:
- PrevLeak Group
- PrevLeak Smart Manhole Covers
- Saferide
- Palettemath
- Qvedic
- Plumber App
- Public Reporting App

Objective:
- Register or prepare each brand for social media presence across major platforms.
- Build a 30-day content schedule with 5 posts per day.
- Keep the tone professional, credible, and brand-led.
- Use the real logo, product imagery, and brand colors for each brand.

Required deliverables:
1. Brand social profile setup plan:
   - Platform list: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Business / channel if relevant.
   - Username strategy, bio structure, profile image guidance, cover image guidance, CTA recommendations.
   - Handle naming conventions for each brand.

2. 30-day content calendar:
   - 30 days total.
   - 5 posts per day.
   - A mix of: brand awareness, product education, trust and credibility, pilot and case study content, app and web CTA content, community engagement, testimonial-style content, and behind-the-scenes operational content.
   - Include post type, caption, CTA, visual suggestion, and hashtag set for each post.

3. Content system:
   - One core content pillar per brand.
   - One recurring content style per brand.
   - A weekly rhythm with educational, promotional, and trust-building themes.

4. Brand-specific guidance:
   - PrevLeak: smart infrastructure, utility accountability, resilient cities.
- Saferide: safety, mobility, reliability, rider and driver confidence, and petrol surge transparency.
   - Palettemath: polished product presentation, professional credibility.
   - Qvedic: innovation, enterprise communication, trusted digital experiences.
   - Plumber App: field operations, responsive service, practical workflow.
   - Public Reporting App: public transparency, service response, issue visibility.

7. Paid audience targeting requirements:
- Saferide paid targeting should prioritize ride-hailing drivers and riders, with copy style aligned to expectations users associate with major platforms such as Uber and Bolt.
- PrevLeak Group paid targeting should prioritize municipalities, water entities, utility operations leadership, and investor audiences.
- Include one investor-positioned stream on LinkedIn focused on growth credibility, portfolio governance, and long-horizon infrastructure value.

5. Visual and brand instructions:
   - Use the uploaded logos and product images as the main visual anchor.
   - Keep colors aligned to each brand palette.
   - PrevLeak should use a clean blue-and-white professional look.
   - Saferide should use a warm white and orange professional look.
   - Palettemath should use a modern restrained premium tone.

6. Conversion and engagement goals:
   - Drive traffic to the website.
   - Encourage app and web CTA clicks.
   - Build credibility for each brand over the 30-day period.

Output format:
- Present as a structured rollout brief with sections for each brand and a 30-day calendar table.
- Make it ready for direct use by a social media manager or marketing team.
