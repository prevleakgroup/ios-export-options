## Handle and Profile Rules
- Reserve the same handle on each platform where possible.
- Prefer lowercase handles with no special characters.
- Use brand logo and color lane consistently.
- Add website link matching brand landing page.
- Add CTA in profile: Book Demo, Request Pilot, or Download Proposal.

## UTM Template
Use this for every social profile link and ad destination:

https://prevleakgroup.co.za/?utm_source={platform}&utm_medium={organic_or_paid}&utm_campaign={brand}_{objective}_{month}&utm_content={creative_name}

Example:

https://prevleakgroup.co.za/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=saferide_driver_leads_aug&utm_content=petrol_surge_card_a

## 30-Day Marketing Run
1. Week 1: Register accounts, complete profiles, and publish 3 introduction posts per brand.
2. Week 2: Launch paid campaigns with 2 creatives per audience.
3. Week 3: Pause low performers, duplicate top ad sets, increase best budgets.
4. Week 4: Retarget engaged users and run conversion-heavy CTA creatives.

## Daily Operations Checklist
- Confirm landing pages are live.
- Confirm ad account billing is active.
- Check lead forms and CTA links.
- Check comments and inbox response SLA.
- Log spend, leads, and CTR daily.

## Security and Access Controls
- Enable MFA on all social and ad accounts.
- Keep owner/admin/editor roles separated by responsibility.
- Store account recovery methods in owner-controlled vault only.
- Never commit passwords, OTPs, access tokens, or recovery codes.

## Final Publish Deliverables
- Live social URL list for all brands and platforms.
- Screenshot proof of profile completion.
- Ad account IDs and campaign names.
- First 30-day performance summary with CTR, CPL, and spend.
