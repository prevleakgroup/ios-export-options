  - Ages 18-45
  - Interests: ride-hailing, urban mobility, commuting apps
  - Behaviors: frequent mobile users, transport apps
- Messaging pillars:
  - Safer journeys
  - Clear support

### Campaign B: Driver Acquisition

- Messaging pillars:
  - Driver earnings stability
  - Petrol surge protection

## 30-Day Cadence
- Week 1:
  - Launch all campaigns with 2 creatives each
  - Verify pixel/conversion tracking
- Week 2:
  - Pause bottom 20% creative performers
  - Add 2 new creative variants per campaign
- Week 3:
  - Shift budget to highest CTR + conversion ad sets
  - Increase retargeting share
- Week 4:
  - Retarget site visitors and engaged users
  - Push strongest lead-gen formats
